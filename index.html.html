About

Editor first.
Story always.

I’m Joe Amato, a video editor and producer focused on creating polished, emotionally resonant work that communicates clearly and always feels human.

My experience in video production started as an undergrad at Penn State University, where I received my bachelor's degree in Film & Video Production and worked two internships during my time there. Since graduating almost 10 years ago, I have consistently worked in freelance video production on projects of all shapes and sizes.

For the past 5+ years, I’ve worked at Jefferson in Philadelphia, spending my first four years producing impactful videos for the philanthropy team before transitioning to the marketing team, where I now create commercial content and support a wide range of marketing initiatives.

My experience thus far has provided me the opportunity to work with C-suite executives, high level donors, local businesses, community activists and so much more.
